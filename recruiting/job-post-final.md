# We're Building a Website That Could Save Lives (Or Destroy Communities)

## The Stakes
We have data showing 1,059 US communities thrive despite being food deserts. Politicians will weaponize this to cut food assistance. Communities could be gentrified. Or we could change how America understands resilience.

## The Mission
Build three synchronized websites that:
- Protect vulnerable communities
- Maintain research integrity  
- Prevent political weaponization
- Work on $50 phones
- Serve 12+ audiences without collision
