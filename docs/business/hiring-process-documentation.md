## 🎯 WHAT WORKED

### The Hook
**Subject Line with 72% Open Rate**: 
"Build controversial civic tech that protects communities"

### The Filter
**Question That Eliminated 60%**:
"Would you kill this project to protect a community?"
- Yes: Advanced to screening
- Maybe: Clarifying questions
- No: Immediate rejection

### The Community Panel
**Most Effective Format**:
- 2 community members (veto power)
- 1 team member
- 1 technical assessor
- Candidate

**Best Question from Panel**:
"My grandmother lives in a food desert. What would you tell her about this project?"

## 📞 REFERENCE CHECK REVELATIONS

### Questions That Mattered

1. **"Describe their biggest failure"**
   - Good: Specific, learned from it
   - Bad: "They don't fail" or defensive

2. **"Would you trust them with vulnerable people?"**
   - Good: "Absolutely, here's why..."
   - Bad: Any hesitation

3. **"How do they handle being wrong?"**
   - Good: "Admits quickly, fixes faster"
   - Bad: "They're rarely wrong"

### Best Reference Quote

## 💡 LESSONS LEARNED

### Do Again
1. Community panel with veto power
2. Living experience requirement
3. Controversy-first messaging
4. Rapid timeline
5. Public salary posting

### Do Differently
1. Record community panels (with permission)
2. Clearer technical test instructions
3. More BIPOC interviewers
4. Backup community panelists
5. Mental health support during process

### Never Do
1. Hire without community input
2. Prioritize credentials over experience
3. Rush reference checks
4. Negotiate below posted salary
5. Compromise on ethics questions
