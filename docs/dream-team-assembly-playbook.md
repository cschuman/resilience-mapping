## 🎭 INTERVIEW PROCESS

### Round 1: The Reality Check (30 min video)

**Opening**: "This project might harm communities. How do we prevent that?"

**Questions for All Roles**:
1. Tell me about a time you were wrong about poor communities
2. What's your relationship with food insecurity?
3. Describe a project you killed for ethical reasons
4. How do you handle public criticism?
5. What's your biggest concern about this project?

**Role-Specific Probe**:
- Product: "CNN is attacking us tomorrow. What do you do?"
- Engineering: "Make this work on 2G in rural Alabama"
- Community: "A tract wants their data removed. Legal says no. Now what?"
- Legal: "Senator threatens investigation. Your move?"

**What We're Assessing**:
- Comfort with controversy
- Community-first mindset
- Technical competence
- Ethical reasoning
- Crisis readiness

### Round 2: The Technical Challenge (2-hour work sample)

**Product Leader**:
Task: Draft three responses to "Your data enables cutting food stamps"
- For community members
- For press
- For Congress

**Technical Architect**:
Task: Design system architecture for:
- 10M requests/hour
- Works offline
- Prevents scraping
- Costs <$1K/month

**Community Trust Broker**:
Task: Plan first meeting with suspicious community
- Agenda
- Participants
- Location
- Trust-building approach

**Frontend Developer**:
Task: Build component that:
- Shows uncertainty visually
- Works on screen readers
- Loads in <1 second
- Prevents screenshot misuse

### Round 3: The Community Panel (90 min group)

**Panel Composition**:
- 2 community members from studied tracts
- 1 team member
- 1 board member
- Candidate

**Format**:
- 10 min: Candidate presents their vision
- 30 min: Panel questions
- 20 min: Scenario roleplay
- 20 min: Candidate questions
- 10 min: Private panel discussion

**Scenarios to Test**:
1. "Fox News just called us poverty porn"
2. "A community leader says we're exploiting them"
3. "Data shows something community says is wrong"
4. "Funder wants to suppress negative findings"

**Community Veto Power**: If community panel says no, it's no.

### Round 4: The Reference Deep Dive

**Questions for References**:
1. Describe their biggest failure
2. How do they handle being wrong?
3. Would you trust them with vulnerable people?
4. What criticism triggers them?
5. Would you hire them again?

**Red Flag Answers**:
- "They've never failed"
- "They don't take criticism well"
- "They're brilliant but difficult"
- "They move too fast sometimes"
- "They care more about the work than people"

## 🛡️ WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

### Crisis Response Protocol

**Level 1: Technical Issue**
- Engineering leads
- Fix within 4 hours
- Community notification if needed
- Post-mortem within 48 hours

**Level 2: Community Concern**
- Community Liaison leads
- Response within 24 hours
- Partner consultation
- Public if necessary

**Level 3: Media Attack**
- Crisis Comms leads
- Response within 2 hours
- Team aligned on message
- Community protected

**Level 4: Legal Threat**
- Legal Counsel leads
- No individual responses
- Team communication locked
- Board notified

**Level 5: Existential Crisis**
- Full team meeting
- Community consultation
- Board involvement
- Consider shutdown

### Team Protection

When team member is attacked:
1. Immediate support call
2. Legal resources provided
3. Mental health support
4. Workload reduced
5. Community rallies

Never alone in crisis.
