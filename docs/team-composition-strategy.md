# 🎯 TEAM COMPOSITION STRATEGY: Health Resilience Mapping Web Project

## Executive Summary
Building a webpage for the Health Resilience Mapping project requires a 15-person interdisciplinary team spanning community organizing, data science, journalism, design, and policy. The core team of 7 specialists must be supported by 8 part-time experts and unconventional voices. Success depends on rotating leadership, community veto power, and prioritizing harm prevention over technical perfection.

## 🔄 TEAM DYNAMICS & GOVERNANCE

### Leadership Rotation Model
```
Weeks 1-4:   Community Liaison leads (relationship building)
Weeks 5-8:   Statistician leads (validation phase)
Weeks 9-12:  Journalist leads (narrative development)
Weeks 13-16: Designer leads (build phase)
Weeks 17-20: Policy leads (launch preparation)
```

### Decision Rights Matrix
| Decision Type | Final Authority | Veto Power |
|--------------|----------------|------------|
| Community harm | Community Liaison | Community Reviewers |
| Statistical methods | Statistician | None |
| Design choices | Designer | Accessibility Specialist |
| Media strategy | Journalist | Policy Advocate |
| Technical architecture | Engineers | Security Researcher |
| Policy messaging | Policy Advocate | Community Liaison |
| Legal compliance | Legal Counsel | Absolute |
| Ethical concerns | Ethicist | Absolute |

### Conflict Resolution Protocol
1. **Community harm > Statistical rigor**
2. **Accessibility > Aesthetics**
3. **Clarity > Completeness**
4. **Protection > Transparency**
5. **Local knowledge > External expertise**

### Communication Structure
- Daily standups (core team)
- Weekly community check-ins
- Biweekly extended team syncs
- Monthly advisory board meetings
- Open Slack with threading
- Documented decisions in GitHub
