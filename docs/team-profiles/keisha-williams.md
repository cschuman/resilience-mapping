Baptist minister, sociologist, and the person who convinced 200+ community organizations to share data after Cambridge Analytica. ## Community Philosophy

### Engagement Principles
1. **Relationships Before Research**: Know names, not numbers
2. **Reciprocity Always**: What does community get?
3. **Consent is Continuous**: Can withdraw anytime
4. **Power Mapping**: Who benefits from this?
5. **Protection First**: If it could harm, don't do it

### Community Process
1. **Initial Contact**: Through trusted intermediary
2. **Trust Building**: Multiple conversations, no asks
3. **Co-Design**: Community shapes the solution
4. **Consent Agreement**: Legal protection documented
5. **Continuous Feedback**: Regular check-ins
6. **Ownership Transfer**: Community controls their data

### Trust Metrics
- Time from contact to consent (longer is better)
- Number of community touchpoints
- Community members with admin access
- Features requested vs. delivered
- Harm reports (goal: zero)

### Communication Channels
- **Primary**: Signal groups with community leaders
- **Secondary**: WhatsApp for broader reach
- **Formal**: Written agreements via DocuSign
- **Emergency**: Direct phone calls

## The Sunday Test

Before any major decision, Keisha asks:
1. Would I preach about this?
2. Would my congregation trust me after?
3. Would God find this just?
4. Would ancestors be proud?
5. Would children benefit?

If any answer is no, she stops everything.
