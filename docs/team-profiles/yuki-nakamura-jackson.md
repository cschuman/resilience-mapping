# Yuki Nakamura-Jackson
## Creative Director & Design Systems Architect

### Professional Summary
Former Design Lead at Code for America who revolutionized CalFresh enrollment with a 34% increase through dignity-centered design. Pioneer of "hostile design resistance" - creating interfaces that actively protect vulnerable users from dark patterns. Published author of "Design as Reparation" (MIT Press, 2024). Holds 12 design patents focused on accessibility innovations.

### Core Stats
- **Years of Experience**: 15
- **Domain Expertise**: Civic tech, healthcare systems, social services
- **Notable Achievement**: CalFresh redesign served 2.3M Californians
- **Education**: MFA Design, Yale | BA Computer Science + Sociology, UC Berkeley
- **Speaking**: Keynoted at Config, IXDA, Design + Research

### Top 3 Leadership Principles
1. **"Beauty is Respect"** - Every pixel communicates worth. Ugly interfaces tell people they don't matter.
2. **"Test with the Most Vulnerable First"** - If it works for someone in crisis, it works for everyone.
3. **"Design Debt is Technical Debt"** - Poor design decisions compound into systemic failures.

### Superpowers
- Can reduce cognitive load by 67% through information architecture alone
- Translates between engineering constraints and community needs fluently
- Sees patterns in user behavior that predict policy impacts
- Creates design systems that survive leadership changes

### Blindspots
- Sometimes over-invests in perfect edge cases (the "last 2%" problem)
- Can be uncompromising on accessibility, causing timeline tensions
- Assumes everyone shares her aesthetic sensibility

### War Stories
- **Victory**: CalFresh - Fought for 3 months to keep the "emotional support" feature that executives called "scope creep." It became the most-used feature.
- **Defeat**: Healthcare.gov - Her original design was "too simple" for stakeholders. The complex version that shipped crashed.
- **Learning**: Realized documentation is power - now creates "design constitution" for each project

### The "Yuki Test"
Any decision must pass: "Would I be proud to show this to my grandmother who was interned?"

### Quote That Defines Her
