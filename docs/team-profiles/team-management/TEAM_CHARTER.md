## Health Resilience Mapping Project

**Date**: January 31, 2025  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Status**: Active

## 🎯 MISSION

**We identify and celebrate communities that achieve good health outcomes despite limited food access, creating a dignified platform that protects and amplifies their stories while providing actionable insights for policy and community development.**

---

## ✊ OUR PROMISE TO COMMUNITIES

1. **No harm, ever** - We will not ship anything that could hurt communities
2. **Community ownership** - Your stories belong to you, always
3. **Dignity first** - Beautiful, respectful representation in everything
4. **Transparent process** - Open about what we're building and why
5. **Veto power** - Communities can stop or change anything at any time

## 🔄 CONFLICT RESOLUTION

### **Step 1: Direct Communication**
- Speak directly with person involved
- Use "I" statements
- Focus on behavior, not character
- Seek understanding first

### **Step 2: Mediated Discussion**
- Bring in neutral team member
- Document the issue
- Identify root cause
- Agree on solution

### **Step 3: Team Leadership**
- Escalate to Amara or Keisha
- Full context provided
- Team input gathered
- Decision documented

### **Step 4: Community Input** (if community impact)
- Present to community panel
- Get community perspective
- Adjust based on feedback
- Implement solution
