## 🎯 HOW WE MADE DECISIONS

### The Decision Framework

**Every Milestone Had to Pass**:
1. **The Mom Test** (Marcus W's mom): Would this help or harm her?
2. **The Beauty Test** (Yuki): Does this bring dignity?
3. **The Technical Test** (Marcus T): Can we actually build it?
4. **The Community Test** (Keisha): Will communities trust it?
5. **The Protection Test** (Sarah): Are we legally/ethically covered?

**If any test failed**: Milestone revised or removed

### The Artifact Criteria

**Every Artifact Required**:
- Clear owner (name attached)
- Specific deadline (date/time)
- Success metrics (measurable)
- Community benefit (explicit)
- Fallback plan (if delayed)
