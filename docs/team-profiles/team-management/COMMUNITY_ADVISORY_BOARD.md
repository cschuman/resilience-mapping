## Why a Community Advisory Board?

We've built a platform that maps 68,000+ census tracts and identifies communities with better health outcomes than expected. We've collected data, run algorithms, and published findings.

**But we haven't asked the communities.**

We've treated communities as subjects instead of partners. That ends now.

The Community Advisory Board (CAB) ensures that:
1. **Communities have power**, not just representation
2. **Decisions serve communities**, not researchers
3. **Stories belong to communities**, not to us
4. **Harm is prevented**, not apologized for

---

## CAB Authority & Powers

### Decision Rights

| Decision Type | CAB Role |
|---------------|----------|
| Feature prioritization | Advisory (30% weight) |
| Community stories | **APPROVAL REQUIRED** |
| Data presentation | **APPROVAL REQUIRED** |
| Public communications | Advisory (with escalation) |
| Research partnerships | **APPROVAL REQUIRED** |
| Community outreach | **APPROVAL REQUIRED** |

### Veto Power

The CAB holds **collective veto power** over any decision that:
- Features a specific community without consent
- Could cause harm to vulnerable populations
- Uses community data in ways not originally consented
- Partners with organizations communities distrust
- Communicates findings in ways that stigmatize

**To exercise veto**: Majority vote of CAB members (3 of 5)

### Escalation Authority

Any CAB member may escalate concerns directly to:
- Project leadership
- Funders
- Public (with 48-hour notice)

## How the CAB Works

### Meeting Structure

**Frequency**: Monthly (2 hours)
**Format**: Hybrid — Zoom with option for in-person at community location
**Language**: English primary, Spanish interpretation available

**Standard Agenda**:
1. Opening & grounding (10 min)
2. Updates from technical team (20 min)
3. Community concerns & feedback (30 min)
4. Decision items requiring CAB input (30 min)
5. Upcoming community engagement (20 min)
6. Closing & action items (10 min)

### Communication Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Response Time |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Signal group | Urgent matters, quick questions | 24 hours |
| Email | Formal communications, documents | 48 hours |
| Monthly call | Deep discussion, decisions | N/A |
| In-person | Relationship building (quarterly) | N/A |

### Decision Process

1. **Issue raised** — By CAB member, community, or technical team
2. **Discussion** — At monthly meeting or async if urgent
3. **Proposal** — Clear recommendation with rationale
4. **Vote** — Majority rules, ties go to status quo
5. **Documentation** — Decision recorded with dissenting views
6. **Follow-up** — Implementation tracked, CAB updated

## Governance Charter (Draft)

### Purpose

The Community Advisory Board exists to ensure the Health Resilience Mapping Platform serves communities with dignity, respects community autonomy, prevents harm, and builds toward community ownership.

### Principles

1. **Nothing About Us Without Us**
   - Communities must consent to being featured
   - Communities control their own narratives
   - Communities can withdraw at any time

2. **Trust is Built in Years, Lost in Seconds**
   - Every interaction matters
   - Promises are sacred
   - Harm is prevented, not repaired

3. **Liberation, Not Charity**
   - We build power, not provide services
   - Communities lead, we support
   - Ownership transfers by 2027

4. **Radical Transparency**
   - All data methodology public
   - All decisions documented
   - All concerns addressed

### Membership

- 5 voting members from mapped communities
- 1-year renewable terms
- Staggered rotation (2-3 new members annually)
- Removal only by 4/5 CAB vote

### Officers

- **Chair**: Elected annually by CAB members
- **Vice Chair**: Elected annually, runs meetings if Chair absent
- **Secretary**: Rotates monthly, takes notes and tracks actions

### Meetings

- Monthly, minimum 10 meetings per year
- Quorum: 3 of 5 members
- Decisions by majority vote
- Remote participation always available
- Meetings recorded with consent

### Amendments

This charter may be amended by:
- 4/5 CAB vote, OR
- Unanimous CAB recommendation + project leadership approval

## The Sunday Test

Before any major decision, we ask:

1. Would Rev. Keisha be proud to preach about this on Sunday?
2. Would CAB members' congregations and neighbors trust them after?
3. Would the ancestors of these communities approve?
4. Would children in these communities benefit?
5. Would we be comfortable if this decision was on the front page?

If any answer is no, we stop and reconsider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why only 5 members? Why not more representation?**
A: Small groups make real decisions. Large boards become rubber stamps. 5 people can have deep conversations. We'll add members as we expand to more communities.

**Q: What if a CAB member's community isn't "resilient" anymore?**
A: Categories change. Membership doesn't. Once you're on CAB, you serve your term regardless of data updates.

**Q: Can CAB members be from the technical team's networks?**
A: No. CAB members must come through community channels, not professional networks. No friends, family, or colleagues of team members.

**Q: What if no one from a community wants to serve?**
A: Then we don't feature that community. Silence is not consent.

**Q: Who pays for the CAB?**
A: Project operating budget. If funding runs out, CAB stipends are the last thing cut.

**Q: Can CAB fire the technical team?**
A: CAB can publicly withdraw endorsement and recommend funders redirect support. This has never happened and we intend to keep it that way.
