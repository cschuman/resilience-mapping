## Project Context

The Health Resilience Mapping Platform emerged from research analyzing 68,170 US census tracts to identify "resilient" communities - places maintaining good health outcomes despite being classified as food deserts. The project discovered 1,059 such communities, though deeper analysis revealed complex patterns around race, policy, and structural inequality.

## Technical Stack

As decided in the Architecture Review Meeting:
- **Frontend**: Vercel deployment, Next.js 15, mobile-first design
- **Backend**: Fly.io hosting, Go services, PostgreSQL via Supabase
- **Performance**: 3-second load on 3G, supports 5-year-old Android phones
- **Security**: Zero tracking, encryption at rest, community consent management
- **Structure**: Three interconnected sites (stories, research, policy)

## Key Principles

1. **"Downtime equals harm"** - System reliability is a moral imperative
2. **"Data without dignity is violence"** - Respectful representation of communities
3. **"Performance is justice"** - Slow sites exclude poor communities
4. **"Nothing about us without us"** - Community ownership of narratives
5. **"Every bug is someone's hungry night"** - Technical failures have human costs
