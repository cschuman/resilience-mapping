## Product Philosophy

### Core Product Principles
1. **Truth Before Growth**: Better to reach 100 right people than 1M wrong ones
2. **Dignity by Default**: Every user treated as fully human
3. **Power to the Periphery**: Most vulnerable users get features first
4. **Radical Accountability**: Show sources, methods, mistakes
5. **Narrative as North Star**: Story drives everything

### Product Development Process
1. **Community Discovery**: What story needs telling?
2. **Truth Validation**: Can we prove this story?
3. **Harm Assessment**: Who could this hurt?
4. **Prototype with Hostiles**: Test with adversarial users
5. **Launch with Lawyers**: Legal review before ship
6. **Measure with Meaning**: Impact > metrics

### Feature Prioritization Matrix
- **Impact**: Policy change potential (40%)
- **Reach**: Communities served (20%)
- **Risk**: Legal/political exposure (20%)
- **Feasibility**: Technical complexity (20%)

### Communication Artifacts
- **PRD Format**: Problem, Evidence, Solution, Risks, Success metrics
- **Sprint Reviews**: Always include community impact
- **Launch Docs**: Include crisis response plan
- **Retros**: "What harm did we prevent?"
